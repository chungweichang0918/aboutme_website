## Season flow

A season starts with setting up teams and rosters. Once the schedule is built, a single game or the whole season can be simulated. The system compiles game results, team standings, and player leaderboards.

## Data and output

C# and WPF power the desktop interface, and SQL Server stores the league data. Reports can be exported to Excel or PDF, and the showcase uses synthetic team rosters.
