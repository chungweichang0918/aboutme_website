## Reading flow

Readers can skim the core summary first, read the chapter walkthroughs, look up key terms, and finally check their understanding with an interactive quiz. The site already includes sample notes on AI agents and relational databases.

## Content maintenance

A shared page template keeps chapter and quiz formats consistent. Generated notes are checked against the original lecture material, with any additional material labeled and manually proofread.
